SLIDE 4 – AI in Practice: Speaker Notes
========================================

AI as a Collaborator
---------------------
Tools like Claude can accelerate your work significantly — helping you explore ideas,
write boilerplate, and debug faster. We use it daily.

Core CS Principles Still Apply
--------------------------------
Algorithms, data structures, systems thinking, and good architecture don't go away.
AI output is only as good as your ability to evaluate it.

The Danger of Autopilot
-------------------------
AI can confidently produce incorrect, insecure, or poorly designed code.
Without a solid foundation, you won't know what you're shipping.

Your Degree Still Matters
--------------------------
Understanding the fundamentals is what lets you direct AI effectively,
catch its mistakes, and build things that actually hold up.
